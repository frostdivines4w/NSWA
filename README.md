## 1. What ShiftSync Does

ShiftSync has a single, focused purpose: helping authorised NSW Ambulance staff work with their own roster, timesheet, and payroll-related information across supported NSW Ambulance and NSW Health workforce systems.

Depending on the version and features available to you, ShiftSync may help you:

- review and match roster, CREWS, eTime, myShift, HealthRoster, StaffLink, and related workforce information
- prepare or populate eTime entries for your review
- estimate pay, allowances, overtime, and financial-year figures
- compare roster lines and projected pay outcomes
- export HealthRoster shifts to a calendar file
- prepare shift-swap forms for your review and manual submission
- receive ShiftSync service messages, update notices, and support information

ShiftSync does not replace your obligation to check your roster, timesheet, pay, claims, leave, or other employment records. Pay estimates and other calculated outputs are guides only and may be incomplete or inaccurate.

## 13. Privacy Policy

### 13.1 Information ShiftSync May Access or Collect

Depending on the features you use, ShiftSync may access, process, or collect:

- **Identity and account information**, such as your NSW Health email address, display name, StaffLink ID, staff identifier, assignment-linked identifier, verification state, and ShiftSync account or session identifiers
- **Subscription and billing information**, such as your Stripe customer and subscription identifiers, selected plan, paid or trial status, billing status, renewal date, cancellation state, and limited payment-method information provided by Stripe
- **Workforce website content**, such as roster and shift details, duty station or work location, eTime fields, leave dates or labels, activity rows, overtime details, crib and meal allowance information, case or incident identifiers, payroll or payslip figures, and other visible content directly relevant to a ShiftSync feature
- **Settings and local workflow information**, such as saved preferences, pay settings, cached verification or entitlement state, automation progress, dismissed message identifiers, downloaded message content, and troubleshooting logs
- **Pseudonymous usage analytics**, such as a randomly generated analytics client identifier, session identifier, extension version, automation type, start or failure event, general error category, general role grouping, pay-settings mode, and paid, trial, or no-access category
- **Support and feedback information**, such as your email address, form responses, logs, screenshots, support scrapes, and any other information you voluntarily send to us
- **Technical and security information**, such as timestamps, request status, browser or extension information, and network information ordinarily received by services contacted by ShiftSync

### 13.2 Information ShiftSync Is Not Intended to Collect

ShiftSync is not intended to collect or monitor:

- unrelated browsing activity or general web history
- patient names, patient clinical records, clinical notes, or medical records
- continuous or device-based geolocation, although workplace locations or duty stations visible in supported workforce systems may be processed
- your NSW Health password, other workplace passwords, or payment-card security code
- personal communications, except information you deliberately include in a support or feedback message

Some relevant workforce information, such as leave labels, case identifiers, or duty locations, may still be personal or sensitive in context. ShiftSync processes that information only where it is visible on a supported page and relevant to the feature you asked it to perform.

Do not include patient information, passwords, full payment-card details, or other unnecessary sensitive information in feedback, logs, screenshots, or support scrapes.

### 13.3 How Information Is Accessed or Collected

Information may be accessed or collected when:

- you open a supported NSW Ambulance or NSW Health workforce page and use a ShiftSync feature

- you open Stripe Checkout or the Stripe customer portal, or Stripe sends subscription and payment events to ShiftSync's entitlement service
- ShiftSync sends limited pseudonymous automation analytics to Google Analytics
- ShiftSync downloads public service messages from a GitHub-hosted message feed
- you open or submit the embedded Tally feedback form, send an email, or otherwise contact support

During a workflow you start, ShiftSync may read and remove HealthRoster site cookies on your device when necessary to recover from a stale HealthRoster session. This may sign you out of HealthRoster and require you to sign in again. ShiftSync does not intentionally send the contents of those cookies to CTT Solutions.
